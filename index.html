Our story

Blankets with a little love looped in.

Wrapped in Grace Co. is a small handmade business run by Stephanie Meninga.

Each blanket is made by hand from thick, squishy chenille yarn: no two come out exactly alike, and that's part of the charm. You choose the size, you choose the colors, and I make it just for you.

Have something specific in mind? Custom colors, custom sizes, patterns and special requests are always welcome. Just tell me what you're picturing.
