About me

Hi, I'm Stephanie.

I'm the maker behind Wrapped in Grace Co., where every blanket is looped by hand.

I make chunky yarn blankets with thick, soft, and strong yarn that feels as good as it looks. Every one is made to order, so no two come out exactly alike, and that's part of the charm.

I started Wrapped in Grace Co. because I love making something with my hands that people can wrap up in, gift to someone they love, or bring home for a new baby.

You pick the size and the colors, from baby blankets all the way to king, and I make it just for you. Custom sizes, patterns and special requests are always welcome.
